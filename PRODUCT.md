# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Members of a small group inspecting shared public-source records on desktop and mobile. A separate private installation retains local queries, briefings and history. The approved brief prioritizes Poland, Europe, Turkey and major global disruptions. This is an operational view, not marketing.

## Product Purpose

Notice important changes and inspect evidence. Map, timeline and bounded Polish queries share filters and reveal coverage gaps. Success means useful briefings, not many dots.

## Brand Personality

Technical, calm, transparent. Polish interface. References from the approved brief: flight tracking, network monitoring and a cleaner control-room tool. Dark mode first; no elaborate visual polish in Phase 1.

## Anti-references

Not a World Monitor clone, SaaS landing page, Christmas-tree map, fake AI risk console, animated hero, decorative card grid or simulated feed.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before interpretation, original links and separate timestamps.
- Unknown, empty, stale, disabled and failed are distinct states.
- One dense readable map/list/evidence workspace.
- Quiet surfaces; colors communicate meaningful category/state/selection.
- System fonts and local assets, no external font/tile telemetry.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The approved roadmap requires keyboard operation and a list alternative to the map. Add visible focus, labels, contrast, text alongside color, reduced-motion support. No specific user disability is assumed.
